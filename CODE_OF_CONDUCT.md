# Code of Conduct

PopRaKo contributors must communicate respectfully, review work on its merits,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sustained
disruption. This applies to repository spaces and project-related private
communication.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository security contact in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation,
or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be
handled confidentially and without retaliation.
